Journal of Vertebrate Paleontology

New fossils from the London Clay show that the Eocene Masillaraptoridae are stem group representatives of falcons (Aves, Falconiformes)

CHARACTER DESCRIPTIONS
	
	1. Lacrimal with well developed, caudally projecting processus supraorbitales: no (0), yes (1).
	2. Lacrimal, ectethmoid, and frontal fused: no (0), yes (1).
	3. Processus basipterygoidei: absent (0), present (1).
	4. Rostrum maxillae roofed by medially co-ossified processus maxillopalatini of maxillary bones: no (0), yes (1).
	5. Processus postorbitalis, wide and with main plane being parallel to the paramedian axis of the skull: no (0), yes (1).
	6. Os lacrimale communicans: absent (0), present (1).
	7. Pterygoid, proximal end dorsoventrally expanded: no (0), yes (1).
	8. Upper beak with sharply hooked tip: absent (0); present (1).
	9. Upper beak, tomial grooves: absent (0), present (1).
	10. Jugal bar very robust: no (0), yes (1).
	11. Upper beak dorsoventrally very deep and mediolaterally compressed: no (0), yes (1).
	12. Mandible, symphysis very long: no (0), yes (1).
	13. Mandible, caudal articular end with marked dorsal projection, which rostrally borders cotyla lateralis: absent (0), present (1).
	14. Coracoid: not as follows (0); processus procoracoideus directed towards hook-like processus acrocoracoideus, both processes are in close contact or even fuse (1). This character cannot be scored for Bathornis and phorusrhacids, in which the processus acrocoracoideus is greatly reduced. 
	15. Coracoid, articulation surface for scapula: concave and cup-like cotyla scapularis (0); shallow facies articularis scapularis (1). Scoring of the character has been corrected for the Cariamidae, in which a cup-like cotyla scapularis is present. 
	16. Coracoid, processus acrocoracoideus extremely reduced: no (0), yes (1).
	CHARACTER DESCRIPTIONS (Continued)
	
	17. Coracoid, well developed foramen nervi supracoracoidei: present (0), absent (1), incisura nervi supracoracoidei present (2). 
	18. Coracoid, extremitas sternalis with distinct medial projection or bulge: no (0), yes (1).
	19. Furcula with weakly developed or completely reduced scapi clavicularum: no (0), yes (1).
	20. Humerus, distal end ventrally inflected with ventrally projecting processus flexorius: no (0), yes (1). 
	21. Humerus, proximal end reduced and comparatively small: no (0), yes (1).
	22. Ulna very short, measuring only about 3/4 of the length of the humerus: no (0), yes (1).
	23. Carpometacarpus, os metacarpale minus distinctly bowed: no (0), yes (1).
	24. Carpometacarpus, portion of trochlea carpalis between processus pisiformis and os metacarpale minus distinctly raised: no (0), yes (1).
	25. Carpometacarpus, proximal end of os metacarpale minus bearing a well-developed tubercle on its ventral side: no (0), yes (1).
	26. Carpometacarpus, distal surface of facies articularis alularis: saddle-shaped or weakly convex (0), markedly convex, ball-shaped (1) 
	27. Carpometacarpus, sulcus tendineus: well-delimited (0), very shallow and inconspicuous (1). 
	28. Pelvis mediolaterally strongly compressed: no (0), yes (1).
	29. Pelvis, well-defined, lateral process dorsal of antitrochanter: absent (0), present (1).
	30. Femur, pneumatic foramen in craniolateral section of proximal end: absent (0), present (1).
	31. Femur, well-developed crista trochanteris: present (0), absent (1).
	32. Tibiotarsus, crista cnemialis cranialis: small (0), well developed and markedly cranially projecting (1).
	33. Tibiotarsus, prominent tubercle distal of pons supratendineus: absent (0), present (1).
	
	CHARACTER DESCRIPTIONS (Continued)
	
	34. Tarsometatarsus greatly elongated and slender, measuring more than the length of the humerus: no (0), yes (1).
	35. Tarsometatarsus, hypotarsus: not as follows (0), block-like, with flat plantar surface, which guides all plantar tendons; crista medialis very low, and sulcus for the tendon of musculus flexor digitorum longus very shallow (1); with two widely separated crests, which encompass all flexor tendons (2). 
	36. Tarsometatarsus, trochlea metatarsi IV forming a plantarly directed, wing-like flange: no (0), yes (1).
	37. Proximal phalanx of hallux very short, measuring less than half of the length of the proximal phalanx of the third toe: no (0), yes (1).
	38. Proximal phalanx of second toe abbreviated, significantly shorter than penultimate phalanx: no (0), yes (1).
	39. Fourth toe, penultimate phalanx abbreviated, not significantly longer than second and third: no (0), yes (1). 
	40. Ungual phalanx of second toe sharply curved: no (0), yes (1).
	41. Ungual phalanges, laterally open sulci neurovasculares: present (0), absent (1).
	42. Phallus: present (0), absent (1). The absence of a phallus is an apomorphy of Neoaves.
